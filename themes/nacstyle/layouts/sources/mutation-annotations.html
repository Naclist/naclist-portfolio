Annotation scope

The source records were derived from Mutations.txt and matched to gene products in Gene.txt. “Protein-changing” combines nonsynonymous and nonsense annotations; “regulatory/flanking” combines upstream and downstream annotations. These categories describe predicted sequence consequences and do not by themselves establish functional effects.
